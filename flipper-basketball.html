Flipper Basketball

About Flipper Basketball

Flipper Basketball brings together the excitement of pinball with the thrill of basketball. Control the flippers to launch the ball toward the hoop and rack up points. It's a perfect blend of quick reflexes and strategic timing!

As you progress, the game introduces new obstacles and challenges. Master the timing of your flips to increase accuracy and achieve higher scores. This addictive arcade-style game will keep you coming back for more!

How to Play

  • Use keyboard keys or tap the screen to control the flippers
  • Time your flips to launch the ball into the hoop
  • Navigate through obstacles as levels get harder
  • Experiment with different flipper positions for better accuracy
