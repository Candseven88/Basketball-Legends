Ragdoll Mega Dunk

About Ragdoll Mega Dunk

Welcome to the Ragdoll Mega Dunk indoor extreme basketball club! This cool ragdoll style basketball simulator makes every trick attempt unique and hilarious.

Throw the ball into the basket and fly there yourself! The physics-based gameplay creates unpredictable and entertaining moments every time you play.

How to Play

  • PC: Use left mouse button to control
  • Mobile: Touch and swipe on the screen
  • Run, jump and throw your character into the basket
  • Every attempt creates a unique trick!
