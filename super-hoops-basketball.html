Super Hoops Basketball

About Super Hoops Basketball

Super Hoops Basketball is a puzzle game where you deliver a basketball to the hoop. Collect stars along the way but be careful not to turn the platforms too much or it'll be game over!

A unique blend of puzzle and basketball that challenges your problem-solving skills.

How to Play

  • Use A and D or arrow keys to move platforms
  • Click on screen to control platforms
  • Guide the ball to the hoop
  • Collect stars for bonus points!
